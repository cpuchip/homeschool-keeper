# Homeschool Keeper - Project Overview

## Vision
An offline-first app/site to plan, task, and journal student homeschool logs to comply with state record laws, ease parental burdens, and help plan daily student education.

## Target Users
- **Parents/Admins**: Mom, Dad, or guardians managing homeschool records
- **Students**: Kids logging their own work (parent-approved)

## Core Problem
Parents need to:
- Track 1,000+ instruction hours per year (Missouri requirement)
- Maintain records of subjects, work samples, and evaluations
- Plan curriculum across multiple children
- Generate reports for compliance

## Key Features (High Level)
1. **Family/Organization Management** - Create family groups with admin and student roles
2. **Student Profiles** - Track each child's education separately
3. **Hour Logging** - Quick, streamlined daily log entries
4. **Subject Tracking** - Core subjects + electives with hour goals
5. **Work Samples** - Upload photos/documents of student work
6. **Planning Tools** - Yearly/weekly/daily curriculum planning
7. **Reporting** - Export to PDF, Excel; compliance dashboards
8. **Offline-First** - Full functionality without internet
9. **Cloud Sync** - Optional paid feature for multi-device/multi-user sync

## Monetization Model
- **Free Tier**: Standalone offline app, single device
- **Paid Tier**: Monthly/yearly subscription for cloud sync, multi-user collaboration

## Compliance Requirements
- **FERPA** - Family Educational Rights & Privacy Act
- **COPPA** - Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (under 13)
- **CIPA** - Children's Internet Protection Act
- Data encryption at rest and in transit
- Parental consent workflows for student accounts
