# Missouri Homeschool Requirements

*Source: [HSLDA Missouri](https://hslda.org/post/how-to-comply-with-missouris-homeschool-law)*

## Hour Requirements

| Requirement | Hours | Notes |
|-------------|-------|-------|
| **Total Annual** | 1,000 | Per school term (up to 12 months) |
| **Core Subjects** | 600 | Must be among the 5 core subjects |
| **At Home Location** | 400 | Of the 600 core hours |

## Core Subjects (Required)
1. Reading
2. Math
3. Social Studies (History, Geography, Civics)
4. Language Arts
5. Science

## Electives (Flexible)
- Physical Education
- Art
- Music
- Foreign Language
- Life Skills
- Any other educational activity

## Record Requirements (Under Age 16)

Must maintain (not submit):
1. **Plan Book/Diary** - Subjects taught, activities engaged in, daily log of hours
2. **Work Samples** - Examples of student work
3. **Academic Evaluations** - Tests, standardized tests, assessments

**Retention**: Keep 2+ years of records; high school records indefinitely

## Age-Based Rules
- **Ages 7-16**: Full requirements apply
- **Ages 16-17**: Provide instruction, but hour/record requirements don't apply
- **Age 17+**: Exempt from all requirements

## App Implications
- Default core subjects should match Missouri's 5 required subjects
- Track hours separately: core vs elective, at-home vs other location
- Support photo/document upload for work samples
- Provide 1,000/600/400 hour progress indicators
- Allow custom school year dates (up to 12 months)
